Prescript Beeper from the Project Moon franchise (specifically Limbus Company).

[__Features__]
1. On-screen pop-up that mimics the 'Beeper' from Project Moon whenever you receive a notification (configurable through the app)
2. Flower Icon at the top right of your screen when you have unread notifications that tells you which apps you have unread notifications from (Marked Apps)
3. Widget that shows the amount of "Prescripts" you have completed (aka how many notifications you have read through the pop-up)
4. Stage unlocks tied to the amount of "Prescripts" you have completed. [Stage 1 - 0 Prescripts]|[Stage 2 - 10 Prescripts]|[Stage 3 - 20 Prescripts]
5. Logs inside of the app that show the last 20 "Prescripts" you received
6. A graph showing the amount of "Prescripts" you received daily/weekly/monthly along with the type of "Prescripts" you received more of and your highest "Prescript" day
7. Additional "Prescripts" that trigger when: [The phone is on low battery]|[The phone is charged]|[Earbuds are on 20% battery]|[A Google Calendar event triggers]
8. Letting you add additional lines for each category of "Prescript" triggered in point 7
